About the shop

A small shop with a long memory

My father opened Aroodh Jyoti in 1990 with two sacks of rice and a hand scale. I still remember sitting on the counter after school, learning every customer's name before I learned my tables.

Today the shelves are bigger, but the way we work has not changed. We weigh honestly, we keep the account book for families who need time, and we never push what you did not ask for.

Gangadhar Yandiger, Owner
